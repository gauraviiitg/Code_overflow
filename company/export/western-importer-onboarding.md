# Western importer onboarding — Bharat Connect

Share this outline with new buyers in the UK, EU, or North America.

## 1. Qualification call (30 min)

- Product specs, target retail or industrial use
- Destination country and preferred incoterm (FOB Mundra vs CIF named port)
- Certification needs (food, textile, CE-marked components if applicable)
- Payment preference (advance + balance vs L/C)

## 2. Sample phase

- Strike-offs or pre-production samples via DHL/FedEx
- Commercial invoice for customs — declare accurate value
- Revision rounds capped in writing before bulk PO

## 3. Production PO

- Written confirmation: MOQ, unit price, lead time, packing, labeling
- Milestone photos; optional third-party inspection
- AQL table attached to PO

## 4. Shipping documents (typical)

- Commercial invoice & packing list
- Bill of lading / AWB
- Certificate of origin (when required)
- Phytosanitary / fumigation (wood packaging, agro)
- COA for food lines

## 5. After delivery

- Defect claim window and evidence (photos, carton marks)
- Reorder forecast for capacity planning
