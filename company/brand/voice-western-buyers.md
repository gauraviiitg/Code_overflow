# Voice — selling to Western buyers

## Sound like

- A **trade desk**, not a tourist brochure.
- Precise on **MOQ, lead time, incoterms, and documents**.
- Calm confidence: “We’ll confirm in writing before production.”

## Lead with

- English documentation, timezone overlap, inspection options.
- Sample programmes with commercial invoice for customs.
- HS codes, packing specs, and who owns QC (AQL agreed upfront).

## Avoid

- Vague “best quality lowest price” claims.
- Over-ornate “Incredible India” marketing on B2B pages.
- Promising FDA/CE approval unless you hold or facilitate specific certs.

## Words that land in UK/EU/US

| Use | Instead of |
|-----|------------|
| Programme / line | “Stuff we make” |
| Proforma / commercial invoice | “Bill” |
| Pre-shipment inspection | “We check quality” |
| Landed cost options | “Cheap shipping” |
| Strike-off / lab dip | “Sample” (alone) |

## Email opener (template)

> Thanks for reaching out. To quote accurately for [market], please confirm incoterm, destination port, target MOQ, and any certification (e.g. food contact, OEKO-TEX). We’ll reply with sampling options and a written lead time within one business day.
