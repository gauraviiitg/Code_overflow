# Western buyer personas (draft)

## 1. UK/EU homeware importer

- Orders mixed containers of textiles + handicraft
- Needs predictable carton marks and retailer barcodes
- Cares about OEKO-TEX narrative and plastic-reduced packing

## 2. US specialty food / ingredients distributor

- COA-led lots, steam-treated spices, private label
- Prior notice and FDA-style labeling questions early
- Price in USD, CIF or DAP to coastal warehouse

## 3. EU garden / stone retailer

- Heavy freight — crating and container load plans matter
- Longer lead times acceptable if photos during carving/finishing
