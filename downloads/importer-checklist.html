Indian handicraft importer checklist

Use this list before confirming a commercial order with any supplier.

  • ☐ Supplier identity and registration status verified
  • ☐ Product SKU and description confirmed
  • ☐ Intended use and destination requirements reviewed
  • ☐ Material, finish, dimensions, and weight confirmed
  • ☐ MOQ and total quantity confirmed
  • ☐ Approved sample or written production reference agreed
  • ☐ Handmade variation and defect criteria agreed
  • ☐ Lead time and required shipment date agreed
  • ☐ Unit packing and master carton specification confirmed
  • ☐ Carton quantity, dimensions, and gross weight confirmed
  • ☐ Quote currency and Incoterm with named place confirmed
  • ☐ Freight and insurance responsibility understood
  • ☐ Applicable tests, labels, and compliance documents identified
  • ☐ Commercial invoice and packing list requirements agreed
  • ☐ Quality check and inspection approach agreed
  • ☐ Payment terms documented in the commercial agreement
  • ☐ Transit damage reporting process documented
  • ☐ Stable SKU reference retained for possible reorder

Requirements vary by product, intended use, destination, and commercial terms. Obtain professional customs or legal advice where needed.
