  2. Review sourcing options and a written quotation

  4. Confirm material, finish, quantity, packing, and relevant requirements
  5. Coordinate sourcing or production and agreed checks
  6. Prepare packing and applicable commercial documents
  7. Ship according to the agreed commercial terms

Quality and handmade variation

Approved samples and written specifications help define the production reference. Small differences in colour, pattern, texture, dimensions, and shape can be natural characteristics of handmade production.

Packing approach

  • Packing is specified for the product, shape, quantity, and shipping method
  • Carton dimensions and weights are published only after physical validation
  • Drop testing is claimed only after a test is completed and recorded

Documents

Applicable documents may include a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin where required, and agreed labels. Requirements vary by product and destination.
